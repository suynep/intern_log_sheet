### Technical "Today I Learned" (TIL)

    - I learned that MVC in Flutter allows quick setup for small apps, but the Controller can become overloaded with logic in bigger apps, making maintenance harder, while MVVM promotes cleaner separation of concerns, easier testing, and smoother UI updates because of reactive state management.

    - After proper study and research on these two different app architecture in flutter I go to know that choosing between MVC and MVVM depends on app complexity: use MVC for simple projects and MVVM for scalable apps with frequent UI updates, leveraging Flutter’s reactive features.
